FROM ISSUE TO RELEASE

Tasks, decisions and time records in one project.

Issues hold task descriptions, priorities, assignees and change history. Boards, backlogs and reports show the same work during planning and project reviews.

01

Set the order and scope of work

Organise the backlog, choose a sprint scope and link issues to a release. Give the team a shared plan they can return to as work progresses.

  • Backlog and issue ordering
  • Sprint goals and estimates
  • Product components and versions
02

Track issue progress

The board shows what is waiting, in progress and done. Workflows define the next steps and the information needed to complete an issue.

  • Boards with filters and swimlanes
  • Custom states and transitions
  • Comments, attachments and history
03

Review progress and recorded time

See project status, release scope and recorded time. Reports and time approvals help you return to the data when it is time to review the work.

  • Project and sprint reports
  • Timer and manual time entries
  • Approvals and correction history

Before you choose Aether

Who is Aether Lemric for?+

For teams that manage projects through issues, work with boards or sprints, and want to connect planning with work history and time records. The Server edition supports installation in your own environment.

Can I install Aether on our servers?+

Yes. Aether Server is designed for self-hosted installation. Your organisation manages the infrastructure, database, attachments, updates and backups.

Do we have to work in sprints?+

No. You can use Kanban boards and the backlog without an active sprint. Sprints are available when your team plans work in iterations.

Can projects have different workflows and access rules?+

Yes. Configure the process and access for a project. Permission schemes determine who can view it and perform individual actions.

How can we bring issues over from Jira?+

Aether supports Jira export imports with mapping and dry runs. Check data coverage before changing tools; importing issues does not automatically migrate add-ons and every part of your Jira configuration.

How do we find out about licensing and deployment?+

Email support@lemric.com. Tell us your team size, how you work and where you plan to install Aether so we can discuss release availability, licensing and deployment scope.
