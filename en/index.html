The same product. Two deployment models.

We are planning Aether as a self-hosted installation and as a service. Your team will choose where it runs.

On your infrastructure

A self-hosted installation for organisations that want to manage their own system and data. Also designed to work without a connection to the vendor's service.

In the Lemric cloud

A SaaS edition for teams that want to use Aether without maintaining their own server. Lemric will manage the infrastructure.

Coming soon

Aether Lemric is in development.

We are starting with the foundations: issue tracking, the workflow engine and time records. The first release is planned for self-hosted installation and as a SaaS service.

We will announce the launch date when work on the first release is complete.
